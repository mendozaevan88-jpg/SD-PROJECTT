About the project

This website was made for a school project. It uses the Astéri & Co. Live Seller Scent Guide 2026 as its content source.

Why stars?

Capella, Polaris, Vega, Altair, and Orion are real stars. Naming perfumes after them gives each scent its own identity in the night sky.

Abstract

Just Like a Star, Keep Shining is a one-page website that presents the 2026 fragrance line of Astéri & Co., a local perfume brand that names each of its scents after a star. The site helps customers who cannot smell a product online by describing each fragrance through its family, mood, scent journey, and best occasions. Visitors can filter the collection by fragrance family and compare how each Astéri version differs from the perfume that inspired it. The project applies HTML for structure and CSS for layout and design to show how a small business can use a clear, responsive web page to guide customers toward the right scent. Customers can also add perfumes to a cart and send their order to the shop through Messenger.
